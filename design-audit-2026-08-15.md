# Just Finds Visual Audit — 2026-08-15

## Scope reviewed

Representative public, owner, administrator, and import routes were examined at desktop and mobile widths: Home, Categories, Jobs, Search Results, Business Workspace, Administration, and Google Import.

## Findings

The existing blue-and-warm-white foundation, search-first hierarchy, rounded controls, and information-rich owner workflow are sound. However, the public experience repeats large white card surfaces and blue gradients with too little brand distinction. Public discovery flows also expose internal validation records visually, which makes the marketplace appear incomplete even though the UI functions correctly.

At mobile width, the public homepage becomes dense, owner listing switcher cards horizontally overflow, and the owner workspace lacks a stronger section hierarchy. The administrator workspace is readable but feels closer to an isolated enterprise dashboard than part of the Just Finds product family.

## Redesign direction

Adopt a unified **local-confidence** system: deep ink/navy, warm canvas, cobalt as the primary action signal, a restrained coral/amber verification accent, crisp editorial typography, subtle map-grid texture, and a repeated location-signal motif. Public surfaces will emphasize decision signals and nearby utility; workspaces will use higher-contrast navigation, modular operational cards, and clear task priority.

## Non-functional constraints

No existing route, role gate, data source, form contract, or ownership behavior will be changed. The redesign must remain responsive, keyboard-accessible, and compatible with current component tests.
