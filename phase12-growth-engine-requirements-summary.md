# Phase 12: Advanced Growth Engine + Business Promotion + Smart Discovery

## Overview
Phase 12 introduces the growth and discovery layer of **Just Finds**, empowering users to discover top-rated local businesses while providing legitimate businesses with transparent visibility and promotional tools.

## Core Modules & Requirements
1. **Featured Businesses (`/admin/featured`)**: Admin-managed priority listings with scheduling (`start_date`, `end_date`), priority weights, and lifecycle states (`ACTIVE`, `SCHEDULED`, `EXPIRED`).
2. **Promotional Campaigns & Business Boost**: Allow businesses to run promotional campaigns and boost visibility across search and category views.
3. **Offers & Smart Collections**: Curated promotional offers and algorithmic/admin-curated smart collections.
4. **Local Trending & Popular Near You**: Discovery algorithms surfacing trending businesses by category, locality, and city.
5. **Business Growth Insights & AI Recommendations**: Actionable analytics and AI-driven growth suggestions for business owners.
6. **Admin Promotion Control & Fraud/Abuse Protection**: Centralized moderation tools preventing promotional abuse and ensuring fair placement.

*Note: As specified in the instructions, custom domain routing, SSL/DNS management, and payment gateway/paid subscriptions are explicitly excluded from this phase.*
